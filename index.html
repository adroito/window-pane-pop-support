Common questions

The app has my city wrong. Window Pane Pop fills in your city from your device location. Check that location permission is on for the app in your phone settings, then reopen the app.

I cannot sign in. Try the sign-in option you used when you created your account (email, Apple, or Google). If it still fails, send us a message below with the email on your account.

Delete my account. Send us a message below from the email on your account and say “delete my account.” We remove your account and its data and confirm by reply.

Report a problem with a job. Send us a message below with the storefront name, the city, and what happened.
